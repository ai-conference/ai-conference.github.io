The Second Workshop On

2026 Theme: Agents, Productivity, and the Future Workforce

WAICI 2026 Junior Faculty Consortium

The WAICI 2026 Junior Faculty Consortium met on Thursday, October 1, 2026, from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM at NYU Stern (Abbe-Bogen Faculty Lounge, KMC 11-185, and Cantor Boardroom, 11th Floor), ahead of the main workshop. Participation was by invitation. Participants presented a paper and discussed their career vision and impact statement with mentors and peers.
