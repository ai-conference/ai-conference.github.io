2026 Theme: Agents, Productivity, and the Future Workforce

WAICI 2026 Program

WAICI 2026, the second Workshop on AI and the Future of Collaborative Innovation, took place at NYU Stern School of Business (Kaufman Management Center, 44 West 4th Street, New York) on October 1–2, 2026. The 2026 theme was Agents, Productivity, and the Future Workforce.
